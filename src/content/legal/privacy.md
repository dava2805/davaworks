# Privacy Policy

Welcome to Davaworks. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is handled.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

Tabula's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

Tabula integrates with Google Drive to store and synchronize your app data directly to your personal Google Drive account. **We do not sell, rent, or share your Google user data with any third parties.** Your data is accessed strictly for the purpose of enabling the app's functionality (saving and loading your workspaces).

## General Data Usage
We value your privacy. No personal data is stored on our servers without your explicit consent.
