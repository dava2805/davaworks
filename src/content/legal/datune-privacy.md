# Privacy Policy for DaTune
**Effective Date:** 1st July 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to DaTune ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use the DaTune iOS application (the "App"). Our core philosophy is to keep your data local and anonymous.

## 2. Microphone and Audio Data
Because DaTune is a music utility app featuring a Tuner, Looper, and Voice Memos, it requires access to your device's microphone.
- **Local Processing:** All audio captured by the microphone is processed locally on your device in real-time.
- **No Remote Storage:** We do not record, transmit, or store your audio data on any of our external servers. Any voice memos or looped recordings you create are saved locally on your device and remain completely under your control.

## 3. Analytics and Third-Party Providers
We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how our users interact with the application. This helps us optimize performance, identify bugs, and improve the application layout.
- **Data Collected:** Anonymous usage data (e.g., views visited, tools used, device models, and general performance diagnostics). This data does not contain personal identifying information or audio recordings.

You can review Google's data safety protocols by visiting the [Google Privacy & Terms Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 4. In-App Purchases
DaTune offers premium features through in-app purchases. All payment transactions are handled securely by Apple via the App Store framework (StoreKit). We do not collect, process, or have access to your credit card information, billing address, or personal financial details.

## 5. Data Security
We value your trust. Because we do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect personal identifying information (such as your name, email address, or phone number), your risk of data exposure is inherently minimal.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or Apple's App Store guidelines. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
