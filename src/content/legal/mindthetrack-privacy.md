# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: July 16, 2026**

Mind the Track! ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information
when you play our game.

## 1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

To provide and maintain the game's features, we use Firebase (a service provided
by Google LLC). We only collect the minimum amount of data required for gameplay
and the global leaderboard:

- **Fictional Names / Pseudonyms:** If you choose to participate in the global
  leaderboard, you will be asked to enter a fictional name. This name is
  entirely up to you and does not need to reflect your real-world identity.
- **Game Scores:** We collect your high scores to rank you against other players
  on the global leaderboard.
- **Device Identifiers:** We may collect anonymous device IDs or installation
  identifiers through Firebase. This is used solely for core app functionality
  (e.g., preventing leaderboard spam, ensuring app stability, and tracking
  technical performance).

## 2. Data Storage and Security

All data collected for the leaderboard is securely transmitted and stored using
Google Firebase.

- Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.
- We do not collect real-world personal data such as your real name, email
  address, phone number, or precise geographic location.

## 3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third-party advertisers or
external companies. Your data is only processed by Google Firebase to run the
global leaderboard and monitor app performance.

## 4. Children's Privacy

Mind the Track! is designed to be safe for all players. Because we do not
collect real-world personal identifiable information (PII), we comply with the
Google Play Family Policies. The fictional names and scores entered into the
leaderboard should not contain any real personal information.

like to request the removal of your leaderboard entry, please feel free to
