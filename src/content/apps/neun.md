**Outsmart the grid in this roguelike puzzle game!**

Build powerful combos, draft game-breaking relics, and survive escalating Antes
to claim the #1 Daily Prestige. Welcome to Neun, a deep, grid-based puzzle game
that blends tactical tile-matching with thrilling roguelike mechanics. Every
swipe matters, and no two runs are ever the same.

## The Core Loop

Swipe and select up to three tiles on the 3x3 grid to build poker-style
hands—Rows, Columns, Diagonals, and L-Shapes. Balance your offensive strikes
against Enemy tiles while strategically collecting Loot, Heals, and Shields. Hit
the Target Score before you run out of Hands to defeat the Ante and level up!

## Deep Tactical Combat

The board is alive and dangerous. Face off against standard enemies, ticking
Bomb tiles, and Corrupted tiles that drain your Discards, Hands, and HP. Use the
Live Prediction engine to calculate your exact damage, healing, and score output
before you commit to a move.

## Synthetic Strategist

Toggle a customizable bot to automate the grind. Fine-tune its playstyle and
weight its perk selection, ensuring your mechanical proxy executes your specific
strategy with surgical precision.

## Game-Changing Relics & Synergies

Surviving an Ante lets you draft powerful, run-defining perks:

- **Blood Pact:** Gain vampirism and a scaling base attack bonus, but sacrifice
  traditional healing tiles.
- **Glass Cannon:** Multiply your score and boost damage, but your Max HP is
  permanently reduced by 25%.
- **Aegis:** Convert all overhealing directly into infinitely stacking
  protective Shields.
- **Gambler:** Stack massive, flat additive score multipliers to your next combo
  hand by strategically burning your Discards.

Combine these Relics to discover hidden, game-breaking synergies!

## Master the Combos

Chain together unique tile combinations like "Sanctuary," "Assassination," and
"Payday". Hold your streak with consecutive hand types to build massive score
multipliers, and trigger cascading tile drops for free plays.

## Daily Prestige & Leaderboards

Do you have what it takes to be the best? Compete on the Daily Leaderboard to
earn stars for reaching the Top 3. Claim the #1 spot to unlock an exclusive,
shimmering Golden Prestige Aura to flex during your runs.
