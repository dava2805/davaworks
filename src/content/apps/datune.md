# DaTune

**Less interference. More inspiration.**

DaTune removes distractions so you can focus on creating. A tuner, metronome,
and looper—unified into one seamless, fluid experience. More than a utility,
DaTune is a design-forward creative space that adapts to your style.

- **Live Effects Per Layer:** Shape each loop with Pan, Pitch, Reverb, and
  Delay.
- **BLE MIDI Support:** Connect external bluetooth midi controllers for
  hands-free looping and performance.
- **Interactive Backgrounds:** Unlock a generative Conway’s Game of Life
  backdrop that evolves as you play.
