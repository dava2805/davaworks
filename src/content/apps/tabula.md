A minimalist workspace designed for absolute clarity. Capture thoughts, organize projects, and stay focused.

## Core Features

- **Distraction-Free**: An interface that gets out of your way so you can focus on what matters.
- **Lightning Fast**: Optimized for speed. No loading spinners, no waiting.

## Seamless Google Drive Integration

Tabula securely connects with your Google Drive to sync your workspaces. Your data remains yours, stored directly in your personal cloud. We never see, sell, or rent your private files.
